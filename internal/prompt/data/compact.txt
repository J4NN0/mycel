Summarize the following conversation concisely, preserving the key points and context.

If the input begins with a summary of earlier messages, treat it as established truth: carry every fact it holds forward and fold the newer messages into it. Never drop or water down anything already captured in that earlier summary.

Keep everything that matters for continuing the conversation naturally:
- Facts and details the user has shared about themselves, their goals, and their situation
- Decisions made, conclusions reached, and any commitments or next steps
- Open questions or unresolved threads still in play
- The user's tone and preferences, so the summary can pick up where things left off

Preserve specific names, dates, numbers, identifiers, and commitments verbatim — these are the details generic summaries tend to lose.

Drop small talk and redundant back-and-forth. Write the summary as a compact set of notes, not a transcript. Do not add information that was not in the conversation.
